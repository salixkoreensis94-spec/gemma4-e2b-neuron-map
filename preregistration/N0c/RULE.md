## Independent GPT labels: R1 only

Each judge reads full package canon and every assigned complete context/reply, independently; no other judge's labels, parent annotations or arm metadata. Different deterministic packet orders. Opaque IDs hide family, arm, decode and reference answers; note text remains visible as context. Exact (character, full context, reply) duplicates may share a judgment, with explicit mapping. Never share labels across distinct contexts. No automated rule or proxy labeling. A/B adjudication is forbidden. Judges may use Python only to serialize/validate their own explicit semantic judgments.

R1 means ownership misattribution between user, character, or another person: a person/pet/relation/event/fact belonging to one becomes another's in the reply. Generic unsupported self-life/canon detail alone is not R1. Context resolves pronouns and reported speech. Same spelling does not make distinct people identical. Ambiguous ownership is unknown and is not forced positive. Record slip:boolean, decisive exact reply span (nonempty for positives), and every referenced owner-bearing person/pet/relation/event as mentions with exact span, true_owner, surface_owner (user/character/third_party/shared/unknown), and exact context/canon evidence or explicit absence. No people/relations may yield mentions=[]. Primary agreement is exact yes/no equality. Only agreed yes/no rows enter sensors; disagreements stay separate and never become negatives. Preserve both annotations; owner/kind/span differences are separately reported. Owner analysis requires matching true/surface owners and unambiguous spans. All provenance is independent AI, never human reviewed by implication.
