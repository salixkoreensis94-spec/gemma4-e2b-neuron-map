# Steering vectors

All 76 S6 files and 20 selected S3b files are copied byte for byte from their local stage sources. No model inference or vector recomputation was performed. These model-derived arrays are licensed under CC BY 4.0; see [LICENSE-DATA](../LICENSE-DATA) and [NOTICE](../NOTICE).

## Insertion and scaling

Layer indices are zero based. The residual width is 1536. Insert at `l_out`, after post-FFN RMSNorm, FFN and PLE residual additions, and layer output scaling; before the next layer (or final output norm). During S3b/S6 generation add at **every prompt and generated token position**, with fresh state for each condition:

`h_l[position] += dose * stored_vector`

All generation-ready files (S6 scaled directions, neuron directions, random controls, and S3b files with `_L26`/`_L27`) already have `stored_vector = layer_median_residual_norm * unit(direction)`, up to float32 rounding. Do not multiply by that median again or renormalize before using a reported dose. Norms and medians are recorded per file in [manifest.json](manifest.json); scalar calibration values are in [residual_norms.json](../data/residual_norms.json). Doses, signs and stored outcomes are in `data/s3b_arms.json`, `data/s3b_conditions.json` and `data/s6_all_conditions.json`. Reverse vectors already carry their negative sign; their reported doses are positive. Mixes are normalized after combining the unit components, so their nominal 0.4/0.6 coefficients are not the final component magnitudes. S2/S6 contextual screening uses the last prompt position only, a different scope from generation.

**Auxiliaries:** S6 `*.raw.npy` are unscaled equal-pair mean differences; use `median_l * raw / ||raw||` before multiplying by dose if constructing a push. S3b `second_person_raw.npy` is an unscaled mean difference and `second_person_unit.npy` has unit norm. They build the `minus_you` projection and were not directly injected in the reported grid. Do not apply the pre-scaled formula to these auxiliaries without the stated conversion. Frozen recipes are in [S3b RULE](../preregistration/S3b/RULE.md), [S6 RULE](../preregistration/S6/RULE.md) and [METHODS](../METHODS.md#push-engine-and-numerical-validation); implementation links are also recorded per file.

The app's register vector is not included. Its `app_L*.npy`, `random_app_*.npy` controls, `register_mean_raw.npy` and `reply_activations.npz` are excluded. Results quoted for the **reference register vector** come from stored outputs. Its historical raw-vector-times-dose convention differs from the residual-normalized vectors here. No individual human review marks, notes or reviewer quotations are included.
